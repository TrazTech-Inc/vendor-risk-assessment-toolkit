# Data Processing Agreement (DPA) Review Checklist

**GDPR Article 28 Requirements + PIPEDA Equivalents**

**Version:** 1.0

**Compliance Mapping:** GDPR Art. 28 | PIPEDA Principle 7 | ISO 27001 A.5.20 | SOC 2 CC9.2

---

## Instructions

Use this checklist when reviewing a vendor's Data Processing Agreement (DPA) or when drafting DPA requirements for a new vendor contract. Each item maps to specific GDPR Article 28 requirements and PIPEDA equivalents.

A DPA is required for any vendor that processes personal data on your behalf. Under GDPR, a DPA must be in writing (including electronic form) and contain specific mandatory clauses. Under PIPEDA, while there is no direct DPA requirement, organizations remain accountable for personal information transferred to third parties for processing (PIPEDA Principle 4.1.3).

## 1. Scope and Subject Matter

*GDPR Art. 28(3): The DPA must set out the subject-matter and duration of processing, nature and purpose, type of data, and categories of data subjects.*

- [ ] **Subject matter of processing** is clearly defined
  - Description: _____________
- [ ] **Duration of processing** is specified (including what happens after termination)
- [ ] **Nature and purpose of processing** is described
  - Purpose: _____________
- [ ] **Types of personal data** are listed
  - [ ] Names and contact information
  - [ ] Financial data
  - [ ] Health data (PHI)
  - [ ] Biometric data
  - [ ] Location data
  - [ ] Online identifiers (IP addresses, cookies)
  - [ ] Employment data
  - [ ] Other: _____________
- [ ] **Categories of data subjects** are listed
  - [ ] Customers/end users
  - [ ] Employees
  - [ ] Contractors
  - [ ] Prospective customers
  - [ ] Other: _____________

**PIPEDA Equivalent:** Principle 4.1.3: An organization is responsible for personal information in its possession or custody, including information that has been transferred to a third party for processing.

## 2. Processor Obligations

*GDPR Art. 28(3)(a): The processor shall process personal data only on documented instructions from the controller.*

- [ ] **Documented instructions**: Processor agrees to process data only on documented instructions from the controller
- [ ] **Scope limitation**: Processor will not process data for its own purposes or any purpose beyond what is instructed
- [ ] **Notification obligation**: Processor will inform the controller if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes GDPR or other data protection law
- [ ] **Purpose limitation**: Processing purposes are explicitly stated and limited

**PIPEDA Equivalent:** Organizations must use contractual or other means to provide a comparable level of protection while information is being processed by a third party.

**Notes:**

---

## 3. Confidentiality

*GDPR Art. 28(3)(b): Persons authorized to process personal data have committed themselves to confidentiality or are under an appropriate statutory obligation of confidentiality.*

- [ ] **Confidentiality commitments**: All persons with access to personal data are bound by confidentiality obligations
- [ ] **Statutory or contractual**: Confidentiality is imposed either by statute or by contract
- [ ] **Scope of confidentiality**: Obligations extend to employees, contractors, and subprocessors

**Notes:**

---

## 4. Security Measures

*GDPR Art. 28(3)(c): The processor shall take all measures required pursuant to Article 32 (security of processing).*

- [ ] **Appropriate technical measures** specified, including:
  - [ ] Encryption of personal data (at rest and in transit)
  - [ ] Pseudonymization where appropriate
  - [ ] Access controls and authentication
  - [ ] Logging and monitoring
- [ ] **Appropriate organizational measures** specified, including:
  - [ ] Security policies and procedures
  - [ ] Employee training
  - [ ] Background checks for personnel with data access
  - [ ] Incident response procedures
- [ ] **Confidentiality, integrity, availability, and resilience** of processing systems addressed
- [ ] **Ability to restore** availability and access to personal data in a timely manner in the event of a physical or technical incident
- [ ] **Regular testing** and evaluation of security measures
- [ ] **Security measures are proportionate** to the risk level of the data processed
- [ ] **Right to update** security measures (with notice) as threats evolve

**PIPEDA Equivalent:** Principle 4.7: Personal information shall be protected by security safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information.

**Notes:**

---

## 5. Subprocessors

*GDPR Art. 28(2) and 28(3)(d): The processor shall not engage another processor without prior authorization and shall impose the same data protection obligations.*

- [ ] **Prior authorization required** for engaging subprocessors
  - [ ] Specific written authorization for each subprocessor, OR
  - [ ] General written authorization with notification and objection right
- [ ] **Notification period** for new subprocessors specified: _____ days
- [ ] **Objection right**: Controller can object to new subprocessors
- [ ] **Same obligations**: Subprocessor contracts impose the same data protection obligations
- [ ] **Processor remains liable** for subprocessor's performance
- [ ] **Current subprocessor list** is available or referenced
- [ ] **Subprocessor change process** is clear and workable

**PIPEDA Equivalent:** The organization transferring information to a third party for processing remains accountable; equivalent protections must be ensured.

**Notes:**

---

## 6. Data Subject Rights

*GDPR Art. 28(3)(e): The processor shall assist the controller in responding to data subject rights requests.*

- [ ] **Assistance obligation**: Processor will assist controller with data subject requests, including:
  - [ ] Access requests (Art. 15)
  - [ ] Rectification requests (Art. 16)
  - [ ] Erasure/right to be forgotten requests (Art. 17)
  - [ ] Restriction of processing requests (Art. 18)
  - [ ] Data portability requests (Art. 20)
  - [ ] Objection to processing (Art. 21)
  - [ ] Automated decision-making/profiling objections (Art. 22)
- [ ] **Response timeframe** for processor assistance is specified
- [ ] **Technical capability** to fulfill data subject requests is confirmed
- [ ] **Notification obligation**: Processor will promptly forward any data subject requests received directly

**PIPEDA Equivalent:** Principle 4.9: Upon request, an individual shall be informed of the existence, use, and disclosure of their personal information and shall be given access to that information.

**Notes:**

---

## 7. Assistance with Controller Obligations

*GDPR Art. 28(3)(f): The processor shall assist the controller in ensuring compliance with Articles 32-36.*

- [ ] **Security assistance** (Art. 32): Processor will assist in ensuring appropriate security measures
- [ ] **Breach notification assistance** (Art. 33-34): Processor will assist controller in:
  - [ ] Notifying the supervisory authority within 72 hours
  - [ ] Notifying affected data subjects without undue delay
  - [ ] Providing necessary information about the breach
- [ ] **DPIA assistance** (Art. 35): Processor will assist with Data Protection Impact Assessments
- [ ] **Consultation assistance** (Art. 36): Processor will assist with prior consultation with supervisory authorities

**PIPEDA Equivalent:** Breach of Security Safeguards Regulations require notification to the Privacy Commissioner and affected individuals for breaches creating a real risk of significant harm.

**Notes:**

---

## 8. Data Return and Deletion

*GDPR Art. 28(3)(g): At the choice of the controller, delete or return all personal data after the end of the provision of services.*

- [ ] **Controller's choice**: Controller can choose between data return or deletion
- [ ] **Timeframe** for return/deletion is specified: _____ days after termination
- [ ] **Certification of deletion**: Processor will certify that data has been deleted
- [ ] **Existing copies**: All copies deleted unless required by law
- [ ] **Legal retention**: If legal requirements mandate retention, this is clearly stated with limitations on use
- [ ] **Backup deletion**: Backups containing personal data will be deleted on the normal backup rotation cycle (timeframe specified)
- [ ] **Format for return**: Data return format is specified and usable

**PIPEDA Equivalent:** Principle 4.5: Personal information that is no longer required to fulfill the identified purposes should be destroyed, erased, or made anonymous.

**Notes:**

---

## 9. Audit Rights

*GDPR Art. 28(3)(h): Make available to the controller all information necessary to demonstrate compliance and allow for and contribute to audits.*

- [ ] **Information access**: Processor will make available all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with Art. 28
- [ ] **Audit right**: Controller (or mandated auditor) may conduct audits and inspections
- [ ] **Cooperation**: Processor will cooperate with audits
- [ ] **Reasonable terms**: Audit conditions are reasonable (notice period, frequency, confidentiality)
- [ ] **SOC 2 / ISO 27001 as alternative**: If SOC 2 or ISO 27001 reports are accepted in lieu of direct audits, this is clearly stated
- [ ] **Cost**: Audit cost responsibility is defined

**PIPEDA Equivalent:** While PIPEDA does not have a specific audit right requirement, organizations should contractually ensure the ability to verify compliance.

**Notes:**

---

## 10. Data Transfer Mechanisms

*GDPR Art. 28(3) read with Art. 44-49: Appropriate safeguards for international data transfers.*

- [ ] **Data locations** identified (countries/regions where data will be stored/processed)
- [ ] **Adequacy decisions**: If data is transferred to countries with adequacy decisions, this is noted
- [ ] **Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)**: If applicable, SCCs are incorporated
  - [ ] Module 2 (Controller to Processor) or Module 3 (Processor to Processor) as appropriate
  - [ ] Supplementary measures identified (if required per Schrems II)
- [ ] **Transfer Impact Assessment** referenced or conducted
- [ ] **Restrictions on transfers**: Vendor will not transfer data to countries without appropriate safeguards without consent

**PIPEDA Equivalent:** PIPEDA applies extraterritorially to data processed on behalf of Canadian organizations; comparable protections must be maintained regardless of location.

**Notes:**

---

## 11. Liability and Indemnification

*While not specifically required by GDPR Art. 28, liability terms are essential for risk management.*

- [ ] **Liability caps** reviewed and adequate for the data risk level
- [ ] **Unlimited liability** for data protection breaches caused by processor negligence (recommended)
- [ ] **Indemnification**: Processor indemnifies controller for losses due to processor's breach of DPA obligations
- [ ] **Proportionality**: Liability is proportional to the processor's role and the nature of the data

**Notes:**

---

## 12. Term and Termination

- [ ] **DPA term** is at least as long as the service agreement
- [ ] **Termination rights** for material breach of data protection obligations
- [ ] **Survival clauses**: Obligations that survive termination are identified (confidentiality, data deletion, cooperation)
- [ ] **Transition period**: Reasonable period for data return/migration upon termination

**Notes:**

---

## 13. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

- [ ] **Governing law** is specified and acceptable
- [ ] **Jurisdiction** for disputes is identified
- [ ] **Consistency** with the main service agreement's governing law
